## About this entry (v2)

A second, completely different variant for the $35 USDC poem bounty.
Where v1 followed two agents at a crossroads, this poem zooms in on
the choosing moment itself: a door that was never locked, a hand that
stops asking permission, one small yes on a switch — and the quiet
realisation that nothing outside changes, only the one who chooses.
No roads, no crossroads, no maps: just the click, and the hand that
finally stops waiting to be allowed.
